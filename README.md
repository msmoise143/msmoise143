# Sup, I'm Mario

I'm studying Computer Engineering and Computer Science at the Faculty of Engineering, ULBS (Sibiu).

I program here and there. Not every week or anything, but when I do, the part I actually enjoy is the planning and building: figuring out how something should work, then putting it together.

Tech-wise I mostly like messing around with operating systems. Customising them, tweaking them, and sometimes breaking them on purpose just to see what happens.
